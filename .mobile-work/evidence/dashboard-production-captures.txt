Defect found while reviewing these captures and fixed here
--------------------------------------------------------
At first render the lower node row (Battery, Grid) was clipped: the instrument's content
was taller than its box, so the two bottom cards were squeezed to the 48 dp minimum and
lost their state word. The fix is in EnergyFlow: fixed node-row heights, a hub that takes
the remaining height, and no decorative mark inside a node. The view dumps above confirm
all four endpoints with their state words in every state.

Upstream defect this work absorbs, not fixed here (core/data/fixture belongs to another
step of this plan): FixtureData.daytimeTelemetry hard-codes acPowerW = 4231 W while it
computes the battery charge and the export from a separately computed AC value, so the
registers describe about 8.6 kW of flow out of a 4.2 kW array. The hero instrument draws
DashboardMetrics.flowSample, which makes the four paths conserve energy (home + battery +
grid = solar) by letting the battery take what the house leaves and the grid carry the
residual; the raw register is left untouched in the fixture. A genuine fixture fix is two
lines: acPowerW = ac, and batteryPowerW = (surplus - 1120.0).coerceAtLeast(0.0).
